Key Physical Takeaways:

  • Rapidity Additivity: Under a longitudinal boost, $y' = y - y_b$. This translates the entire rapidity distribution $dN/dy$ left or right like a rigid body, preserving its width and shape.
  • Pseudorapidity Deformation: Pseudorapidity $\eta$ does not transform linearly because polar angle $\theta$ transforms non-linearly. The distribution $dN/d\eta$ skews and changes peak heights under boosts.
  • Lorentz Beaming: Observe on the canvas how particles bunch forward (to the right for $\beta > 0$) as the boost increases. This is the relativistic searchlight effect!
