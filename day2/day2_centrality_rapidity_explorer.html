Glauber Collision Geometry Insights:

  • Collision Centrality: Centrality scales geometrically with $b^2$. Small $b$ yields high nucleon overlap and large particle multiplicity ($dN/d\eta$).
  • Two-Component scaling: Multiplicity scales as a hybrid mix of soft participant scaling ($N_{\mathrm{part}}$) and hard binary collisions scaling ($N_{\mathrm{coll}}$): $dN_{ch}/d\eta \propto \alpha \frac{N_{\mathrm{part}}}{2} + (1-\alpha) N_{\mathrm{coll}}$.
  • Spectators vs Participants: Participating nucleons overlap and produce particles in the detector barrel, while spectators continue along the beam pipe into zero-degree calorimeters (ZDCs).
