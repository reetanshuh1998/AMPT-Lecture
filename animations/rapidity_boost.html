Why Rapidity is Special (Sahoo Chapter 5):

Classical Velocity Addition: v_total = (v₁ + v₂) / (1 + v₁v₂/c²) [Highly Non-linear ❌]

Rapidity Addition: y_total = y₁ + y₂ [Linear ✓]

Hyperbolic Analogy: The relativistic velocity sum reduces to the hyperbolic tangent identity:
tanh(y₁ + y₂) = (tanh y₁ + tanh y₂)/(1 + tanh y₁ tanh y₂), making rapidity the natural additive coordinate for boosts along the beam axis.
