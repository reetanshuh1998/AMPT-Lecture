What you are seeing: The green region is the free middle of the QCD string — it breaks uniformly (same dN per Δy), producing the flat plateau in dN/dy. The orange region is attached to valence quarks of the original nucleons — it fragments differently, producing a falling distribution. As energy increases, y_beam = ln(√s/mₚ) grows, the string gets longer, the free middle gains more Δy slices — each with the same number of particles from quantum tunneling — so the plateau gets wider while its height barely changes.
