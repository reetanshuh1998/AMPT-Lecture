Textbook Verification: Hyperbolic Rotations and Lorentz Coordinate Axis Skewing

Spacetime Lorentz Transformations (Sahoo Chapter 5):
• Notice how the boosted coordinates axis x' and time axis ct' **skew symmetrically** towards the light cone as β increases.
• The light cone is invariant (speed of light c is identical in all reference frames).
• This coordinate axis rotation is a **hyperbolic rotation** in Minkowski space, contrasting with standard trigonometric rotations in Euclidean space.
