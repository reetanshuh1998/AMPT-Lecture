Thermal Emission vs. Perturbative Hard Scattering

Transverse momentum ($p_T$) spectra are shaped by two fundamentally different physics regimes:

dN/dp_T ∝ A * e^{-m_T/T_{eff}} (Soft) vs. B * (1 + p_T/p_0)^{-n} (Hard)

Soft Production: Thermalized hadrons evaporate from the expanding fireball, dominating the low-$p_T$ regime (visualized as isotropic blue expanding particles).
Hard Production: High-$p_T$ parton-parton collisions yield back-to-back jets, creating a power-law tail that dominates at high $p_T$ (visualized as high-speed red particle jets). Slide the Hard Scatter Probability up to see the power-law tail lift!
