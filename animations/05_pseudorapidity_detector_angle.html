Mathematical Trick (η definition):

  • Transverse Barrel: At $\theta = 90^\circ$, particles shoot straight out. $\tan(45^\circ) = 1.0 \Rightarrow \eta = -\ln(1.0) = 0.0$.
  • Logarithmic Expansion: As $\theta \to 0^\circ$ (forward beam), $\tan(\theta/2) \to 0 \Rightarrow \eta \to +\infty$.
  • Backward Symmetry: As $\theta \to 180^\circ$ (backward beam), $\tan(\theta/2) \to \infty \Rightarrow \eta \to -\infty$.
