Physical Principles (Slide 4):

  • Wave-Particle Duality: de Broglie's theorem asserts that a particle of momentum p possesses an equivalent wavelength λ = h/p = hc/(pc).
  • Probing Resolution Limit: To resolve a physical object of scale d, the probing wavelength must satisfy λ ≤ d.
  • Energy Demand: Higher spatial resolution requires shorter wavelengths, which demands increasingly energetic accelerators (SPS $\to$ RHIC $\to$ LHC).
