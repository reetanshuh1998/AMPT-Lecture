Connection to Experiment:

Matui-Satz Mechanism: Heavy Quarkonium suppression ($R_{AA} < 1$) in heavy-ion collisions serves as a direct QGP thermometer. Because $\Upsilon$ is smaller than $J/\psi$, it survives at higher temperatures, illustrating sequential suppression at RHIC and LHC.
