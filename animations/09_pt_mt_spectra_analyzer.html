Formula (2D model): T_eff = T_th + 1/2 · m_0 · v_flow^2
Key Physics: In relativistic heavy-ion collisions, the extreme initial pressure forces the fireball to expand radially outwards at velocity v_flow. A produced hadron's transverse velocity is a superposition of this collective radial flow and its random thermal motion: v = v_flow + v_th. This boosts the kinetic energy of heavier particles more because they carry more mass (E_k = 1/2 m v_flow^2). As a result, the effective slope (temperature T_eff) rises linearly with mass.
Observe: 1. When plotted vs p_T, heavy protons have a distinct "radial flow shoulder" (flatness at low p_T) while light pions fall off exponentially. 2. Toggle to m_T − m_0: the curves become straight lines on the log scale at low-mT, with slopes directly equal to −1/T_eff. 3. Increase v_flow to see the proton slope flatten dramatically, separating it from the pions.
