Physical Context (Sahoo Chapter 5):

  • Classical Addition (v_total = Σv_i): Grows linearly without bound. With 10 boosts of 0.1c, the speed reaches 1.0c, and exceeds it at 11 boosts, violating causal structures of spacetime.
  • Relativistic Addition (w = (v+u)/(1+vu)): The denominator term vu/c² acts as a relativistic dampening factor. The combined speed is constrained below the speed of light c.
