⚛ Heavy-Ion Collision Simulator

Interactive visualization of Au+Au collision stages in AMPT

Approach Collision QGP Hadronization Freeze-out

This animation shows the stages of a heavy-ion collision as modeled by AMPT:

Two Lorentz-contracted gold nuclei approach → overlap and form a hot QGP fireball → the fireball expands and cools → quarks hadronize into final-state particles → particles freeze out and stream freely to detectors.
